# Hackstra — Research → Requirements Matrix

**Date:** 2026-10-21 · Lessons drawn from the vendor references named in the
product brief (PowerSchool, Blackbaud, Fedena) as product-design input —
vendor case studies are marketing material, not independent benchmarks, and
no vendor outcome is claimed as a Hackstra result. "Exists today?" is graded
against the repository and the live demo on 2026-10-21; evidence detail in
`PRODUCT_AUDIT.md` (§2 numbering matches `CASE_STUDY.md` gap rows where
cited). Priorities: **P0** = do now (core promise or security), **P1** =
next (clear buyer value), **P2** = later (valuable, not blocking a sale).

## Reading of the matrix

- **Only two P0 items**, and both are already in motion: **circulars** (#9/#14)
  and **closing the two authorization gaps** (#17).
- The brief's platform/school separation (#15) needs **no new role system** —
  the roles and the settings/audit split already exist; the work is one
  dedicated platform home.
- Everything PowerSchool-flavoured (#1–#6) forms one sequenced feature —
  **admissions** — and none of it blocks selling the current product, whose
  staff-side student creation already works.
